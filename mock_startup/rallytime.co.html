Our story

Built by a parent, for parents.

Rallytime was founded in 2023 by Meg Alvarez, a Provo mom of three who was tired of juggling six different signup sites every season. We're a small, bootstrapped-then-seed-backed team on a mission to make it effortless for families to find great things for their kids to do.
