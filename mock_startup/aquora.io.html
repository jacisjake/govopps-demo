The problem

U.S. utilities lose ~6 billion gallons of treated water a day.

Sense

Battery-powered acoustic nodes clamp onto existing infrastructure and listen for the signature of a leak.

Locate

Our models correlate signals across the network to localize leaks to within a few meters, ranked by severity.

Prioritize

Crews get a work queue: which leaks to fix first, by water saved and rupture risk.

Origins

Born from water research, built for rural America.

Aquora spun out of the Utah Water Research Laboratory at Utah State University in 2022. We're headquartered in Price, Utah — a rural community — because the water-loss problem is hardest, and least served, in small and rural systems. Our early work was supported by an EPA water-technology pilot and a Department of Energy efficiency program.
