The problem

Nurses spend up to a third of every shift charting.

Ambient capture

Cadence turns bedside conversation and vitals into structured nursing notes, flowsheet entries, and shift summaries automatically.

EHR-native

Drafts write back into Epic and Cerner in the nurse's own workflow — no separate app to babysit, no double entry.

Built for the floor

Designed with med-surg and ICU nurses for the realities of a 12-hour shift: interruptions, handoffs, and constant reprioritization.
